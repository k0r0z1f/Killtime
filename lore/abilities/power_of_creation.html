Entity Resonance Log: Kinetic Vitality (Mina-A)

Mapping the fluid dynamics, organic creation, and untamed heat of instinctual magic.

Phase I: The Gilded Cage & The Dormant Breath [RC1 Data]

1761 to 1770 HSC - The Silent Restraints

Unlike Lucas's cold calculus, Mina-A's connection to the arcane is the natural heartbeat of the world. Her power of creation taps into all arcane fields simultaneously, not through rigid equations, but through pure, fluid impulse. Raised in a gilded cage and protected by the terrestrial shadow war, she was forced to suppress this boundless energy. The restraints on her power are not mathematical firewalls; they are heavy, emotional floodgates holding back a rushing torrent of kinetic vitality that desperately wants to breathe and expand.

System Note: The organic paradox. Her magic is a living, breathing current. The primary danger is not that she lacks control, but that her natural momentum is too immense for a mortal vessel to contain without constant, exhausting emotional restraint.

Phase II: Feral Grace & Fluid Momentum [RC1 Data]

1771 HSC - The Central Institute (The Archive Leak)

When the heavy doors of the archive open and physical danger approaches, her suppressed heat bleeds into the physical realm. Her magic manifests not as geometric grids, but as raw kinetic momentum. She does not calculate trajectory; she rides the natural slipstream of the world's energy. Her movements become a fluid dance of devastating grace, her vitality bleeding outward to organically disrupt the sterile environment and alter the battlefield through pure instinct.

Power Progression: The floodgates open. Her presence alone generates a thermal and kinetic slipstream, proving her magic is an instinctual expression of life rather than a programmable science.
1772 HSC - The Phalanx Siege

During the chaos of the city streets, her power redlines. Desperate to unleash her feral grace, she acts as the rushing vanguard of the group. She doesn't just attack; she organically creates the path forward, her heat and kinetic energy melting through the enemy's formation like a torrential river breaking a dam. It requires Lucas's cold anchor to temporarily cool her boiling vitality before she tears her own ligaments apart from the sheer force of her own impulse.

System Note: The kinetic burden. Her impulse to create and move is absolute, requiring an external thermodynamic anchor to prevent her own vitality from burning her physical body out.

Phase III: The Tapestry of Creation [Draft Data]

1772 HSC - The Subterranean Crucible

As they journey deeper into the mysteries of the Labyrinth, the true nature of her power reveals itself. It is not limited to physical destruction or kinetic bursts; she taps into the very breath of reality. She instinctively feels the fluid dynamics of all arcane fields—earth, water, wind, and fire—treating them not as separate elements, but as interwoven threads in a single, organic tapestry. When she pours her untamed heat into Lucas to amplify his shields during the avalanche, she is not merely transferring energy; she is breathing life into his dead equations, harmonizing the chaotic magic of the world into a singular, protective heartbeat.

Power Progression: The unbound creator. Her magic is the raw, unrefined essence of the cosmos, restrained only by her mortal form and her own deep-seated fear of hurting those she loves with her boundless momentum.
