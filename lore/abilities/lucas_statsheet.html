Background Summary (Pre-Institute)

Subject Lucas-A is fifteen years old and entirely unanchored. Living in complete emotional and systemic isolation following the traumatic experiments of his childhood, he is a tightly coiled spring of quantum mathematics trying desperately not to detonate. He has just recently discovered the "Acoustic Void" (Thomas) and the "Kinetic Warmth" (Mina).

His magic is heavily suppressed. He treats his own biology as a hostile threat. Without the Central Institute's rigorous physical and arcane conditioning, his body cannot handle his mind's output. Every equation he runs threatens to overload his fragile nervous system.
