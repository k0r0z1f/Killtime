Birth Year

Born in 929, the Queen of Elves is 842 years old in 1771, embodying centuries of wisdom and experience as the ruler of Haliriel.

Role/Background

The Queen of Elves, Aphyrosia, is the sovereign ruler of Haliriel, leading the High Elves Council and ensuring the prosperity and stability of the Elven Realm. She is deeply involved in ancient prophecies that shape the future of the Elves and their alliances with other races.

Personality Traits

As a leader, she is commanding, regal, and authoritative, with a deep sense of responsibility to her people. She is wise, diplomatic, and protective, but also cryptic and calculated, often keeping her true intentions hidden behind her calm exterior.

Abilities/Magic

She possesses mastery over Air Magic, including nature-based powers, healing, and control over plant life. Her enhanced senses and vast arcane knowledge make her an unparalleled force in both combat and diplomacy.

Alliances/Clans/Factions

She is closely tied to Haliriel, the City of Elves, and is a leader among the High Elves. Her role in ancient prophecies ties her to larger events beyond her realm, including dealings with other races and potential allies for the future.

Appearance

The Queen is tall and regal, with silver hair intricately braided and adorned with a crown of leaves and jewels. Her piercing green eyes reflect her wisdom and power, while her robe, woven from the very essence of the forest, signifies her deep connection to nature.

Character Arc/Goal

Her journey involves leading the Elven people through political and existential challenges. She is seeking to protect her people and secure their future, all while guiding Lucas, Mina, and others involved in the prophecy, revealing deeper truths as the story unfolds.

Secret

The Queen harbors hidden knowledge about the prophecy, secret alliances, and the true nature of the Watcher. She may deceive those closest to her, including Candice and Jarek, for the greater good of the Elven cause. Few know her true name: Aphyrosia.

Combat Training

With high combat training in elven warfare, archery, and both defensive and offensive magic, the Queen is an expert strategist and warrior, capable of leading her forces in battle.

Inventory

Her inventory includes elaborate ceremonial robes, an enchanted staff imbued with ancient powers, a collection of rare elven relics, and a small pouch of rare healing herbs to aid in restoration and protection.
