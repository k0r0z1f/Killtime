Birth Year

Lucas was born in 1754, making him 17 years old.

Role/Background

Hero of Brum'korath and former prisoner who survived the Cults War and the Disciple of Earth's rampage. He is the main protagonist and one of the "Children of Hybris," central to an ancient prophecy. A survivor of horrific medi-laboratory experiments from birth to age 11, he is a declared outlaw of the Central Lands following the Kingston incident. He recently journeyed up the Watcher, destroyed a massive Guardian Beast with pyrokinesis, and led the excavation of the Temple of Crystals. During Chapter 33, he penetrated the hidden "Tombs of the First" and made a chilling discovery: an ancient sarcophagus bearing his own name, Lucianae.

Personality Traits

Determined, analytical, introspective, and highly calculating. He is practical, cautious, curious, and incredibly calm under intense pressure. While burdened by hidden truths, laboratory trauma, and the crushing weight of responsibility, his recent victories have forged a more resolute, fierce protector. He possesses a genius-level intellect, capable of solving complex puzzles and adapting quickly. Vulnerable and deeply loving toward Mina, he is willing to push himself to the absolute brink to keep his chosen family safe, yet he carries heavy guilt when his power threatens allies (as seen when he accidentally endangered Candice).

Abilities/Magic Analysis

  • Telepathy (Advanced): Shares a permanent, deepening bond with Mina. Capable of deep mind reading, extracting suppressed memories, and mind control. He can intuitively navigate mental "static" and detect lies or hidden motives, successfully verifying Thorne's complex scientific claims via surface-level probing.
  • Pyrokinesis (Advanced): Transitioned from accidental flare-ups to lethal, precise control. He has learned to "embrace the fire," shaping flames into pillars, sweeping arcs, and concentrated blasts to incinerate targets like the Guardian Beast.
  • Telekinesis (Intermediate / Amplified Advanced): Can hurl heavy projectiles at high velocities. When his mind synchronizes with Mina, his telekinesis is massively amplified, allowing him to create forcefields or unleash massive concussive shockwaves to shatter rock formations.
  • Arcane Energy Sensing: Without needing technological tools, Lucas can "feel" the invisible currents and energy flows between magical objects, allowing him to solve ancient arcane puzzles purely through intuition and sensory tracking.
  • Clairvoyance (Novice/Unpredictable): Experiences vivid, visceral visions of the past, including traumatic first-person views of his abusers.
  • Intellect & Magi-Tech Aptitude (Genius): Uncanny ability to understand and manipulate ancient arcane science, out-pacing seasoned researchers.

Alliances/Clans/Factions

Aligned intimately with Mina and Thomas. Allied with Queen Aph of Haliriel, Chief V, and the Nythari Tribe. Regarded as a prophesied savior by the Cult of the Sent Ones. Currently in an uneasy, tense collaboration with Dr. Thorne (the Researcher). He is an enemy of the state to the World Police, and actively hunted by the Cult of the Unbound and the Empire.

Appearance

5'10", lean but hardened by constant battle. He has disheveled light-brown hair and serious brown eyes that reflect deep emotional strain and intelligence. He typically wears a coat, boots, and a worn satchel containing his research. Fused to his wrist is an ancient, obsidian-like relic that occasionally pulses with energy and alien consciousness.

Character Arc/Goal

Lucas's arc has shifted from surviving his past to actively seeking to master his immense powers and understand his destiny. He aims to uncover the true origins of the relics and prevent the catastrophic destabilization of the arcane field threatened by the Temple's bursting crystals. Ultimately, he seeks to navigate the political treachery around him, defeat the Empire's Disciples, and uncover his direct connection to the ancient Sent One known as "Lucianae."

Secret

He hides the severe guilt of nearly hitting Candice with a wayward telekinetic shockwave. He discovered through a hacked interface that his DNA has a <0.0001% mismatch with the relic's intended host. He harbors the horror of a clairvoyant vision where he relived his childhood torture through his abuser's eyes. Most recently, when approaching the sarcophagus of "Lucianae", his dormant relic finally awakened to telepathically whisper: "Owner... near..."

Combat Training

Operates as the strategic anchor of his team's combat formations. Utilizes tactical genius, using his environment and combining powers (like thermal shock) to defeat foes vastly larger than himself. Transitioned into a devastating mid-to-long range combatant, relying on high-velocity telekinesis and lethal pyrokinesis.

Inventory

810 doumis; rare battery hybrid magic-tech (worth 20,000 doumis); travel clothes; satchel with notes, theories, and relic data; a homemade magi-tech parasitic device; and the fused obsidian relic on his wrist.

Mina

Best friend and romantic partner. Their profound telepathic and emotional link acts as Lucas's primary grounding force, capable of amplifying his powers exponentially when their minds sync (such as their combined effort to clear the Temple's rubble).

Thomas

Best friend and physical vanguard of the group. Wields an adaptive piston-gauntlet relic. Lucas relies on Thomas's brute strength and protective instincts to anchor his long-range strategy.

Candice

Nythari warrior and trusted guide. Lucas harbors quiet guilt after a unified telekinetic blast he unleashed with Mina almost struck Candice with lethal debris.

Dira

Scholar and friend. Lucas used his telepathy to uncover that she was a coerced spy for the Unbound, ultimately choosing to forgive her and keep her as an asset to their group.
