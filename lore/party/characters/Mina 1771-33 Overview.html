Birth Year

Mina was born in 1754, making her 17 years old at the time of the story. Her youth contrasts sharply with the massive, world-altering destiny placed upon her shoulders, yet she handles it with a maturity far beyond her years.

Role/Background

Mina is the adopted daughter of a powerful businessman and secret criminal boss. However, her true origins tie back to her miraculous survival as an infant during a devastating arcane storm that destroyed her hospital. She is a central figure in the prophecy as one of the "Children of Hybris." Serving as the group's scout and voice of reason, her role drastically deepened during the expedition to the Watcher. She actively assisted in excavating the sealed Temple of Crystals and made a chilling discovery deep within the mountain's "Tombs of the First": an ancient obsidian sarcophagus bearing her ancient name, Minalia.

Personality Traits

Mina is defined by her calm, composed, and highly logical demeanor. She is deeply empathetic and acts as the grounding emotional anchor for Lucas. Fiercely brave and observant, it was her sharp intuition that solved the light-refraction puzzle in the Temple of Crystals by noticing the lens-like facets on the white monolith. While usually in control, she recently experienced profound shock, guilt, and horror when a joint telekinetic effort with Lucas violently scattered rubble and nearly crushed Candice.

Abilities/Magic

  • Super Speed & Agility: Moves with blinding quickness, capable of bypassing standard defenses and striking with devastating kinetic force.
  • Hyper Senses & Observation: Can process environmental data at extreme speeds; she was the only one to notice the optical properties of the ancient white crystal.
  • Telepathic Link: Maintains a permanent, seamless mental bond with Lucas. They can communicate complex strategies silently and instantaneously.
  • Arcane Amplification (Sync): By syncing her mind with Lucas and drawing inward, she can weave her raw, untamed magical energy around his telekinesis. This exponentially amplifies his power, allowing them to shatter massive rockslides with concussive shockwaves.

Alliances/Clans/Factions

Mina is devoted entirely to her found family: Lucas and Thomas. She has forged a strong, respectful bond with Candice (Nythari Tribe) and a cautious alliance with Dira. Through the prophecy of the Children of Hybris, she is indirectly tied to the Cult of the Sent Ones, and has recently gained the protective oversight of Queen Aphyrosia of Haliriel.

Appearance

Mina possesses a striking, athletic build marked by scars from her countless battles. Her bright blonde hair is usually kept practical for combat, drawing attention to her piercing, perceptive green eyes. In Ceylan’s strange, psychic visions of the past, her spiritual predecessor was described as impossibly tall, thin, with wavy liquid-like skin and multi-jointed fingers. Currently, her most prominent feature is the ancient, silvery relic tightly fused as a band around her ankle, which has recently begun to pulse with a rhythmic, ethereal glow.

Character Arc/Goal

Mina’s core drive has always been to protect Lucas and secure their freedom from those trying to use them. However, her arc has rapidly shifted toward self-discovery. Confronted with the sarcophagus of "Minalia" and the sudden awakening of her ankle relic, she is desperate to understand her true origin. She must reconcile her protective instincts and independence with the terrifying possibility that she is the reincarnation of an ancient, god-like being.

Secret

Mina hides the deep, gnawing guilt she felt after her combined magic with Lucas nearly killed Candice with a piece of high-velocity rubble. More pressingly, she shares a terrifying secret with Lucas: inside the Tomb of the First, the dormant consciousness within her silvery ankle relic finally awakened, telepathically whispering the chilling phrase: "Owner... near..."

Combat Training

Mina relies on her natural athleticism and superhuman speed. She employs hit-and-run acrobatics, making her a master of evasion and rapid, armor-cracking strikes. Recently, her combat style has evolved to incorporate synergistic "tag-team" magic with Lucas, acting as a physical distraction and a magical battery for his telekinetic offense.

Inventory

Mina carries 90 doumis, highly practical travel clothes, and essential survival gear. She is armed with a compact dagger for close combat. Her most significant possession is the silvery relic fused to her ankle—an artifact that has recently activated and resonated with the ancient dwarven ruins. She also carries a small good-luck charm/token given to her by the Nythari villagers.

Lucas

Mina's best friend, lover, and telepathic partner. She is the steadying force to his inner turmoil. When they join minds in combat, they can generate massive, concussive bursts of energy. They share a profound understanding of each other's fears and burdens.

Thomas

A trusted ally and the group's steadfast physical vanguard. Mina relies on Thomas to hold the line while she utilizes her speed. She felt immense gratitude toward him when he successfully blocked the rogue debris that almost struck Candice.

Candice

The Nythari warrior and guide. Mina respects Candice deeply and shares a cooperative dynamic with her on the field. Mina currently harbors a knot of intense fear and regret after the telekinetic blast she fueled almost resulted in Candice being crushed by a boulder.

Dira

Mina regards the scholar with a mixture of friendship and protective caution. While wary of Dira's past betrayal, Mina acts as a physical buffer for her in dangerous environments like the Temple of Crystals, recognizing Dira's vulnerability.
