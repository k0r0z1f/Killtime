Birth Year

Dr. Thorne's birth year is unknown. He is an older, established scientist with decades of research under his belt, having been active since Lucas's infancy.

Role/Background

Formerly known only as "The Researcher," Dr. Thorne was the head of the Kingston medi-laboratory where he subjected a young Lucas to horrific experiments. Now operating independently after the fall of the Kingston regime, he hired the mercenary Kael and the historian Elara to force his way into the sealed Temple of Crystals using heavy excavation machinery. He claims his past atrocities were "necessary sacrifices" to stop the Disciples of the Empire.

Personality Traits

Thorne is cold, clinical, and completely lacks empathy, viewing even human suffering as a mere data point. He is highly arrogant and condescending, explaining complex arcane physics as if speaking to children. However, he is remarkably pragmatic; he readily sets aside past animosities to collaborate with Lucas when a puzzle requires both his technology and Lucas's magical intuition.

Abilities/Magic

While he possesses no innate offensive magic, Thorne is a genius in arcane physics and magi-tech engineering. Furthermore, he possesses a formidable mental defense. When Lucas attempted to telepathically probe his mind, Thorne's thoughts were shielded by a buzzing, disruptive "static," and he was immediately aware of the intrusion.

Alliances/Clans/Factions

Thorne previously served the Kingston Regime but now operates as a rogue element. He leads a three-person faction (himself, Kael, Elara). Currently, he has forged a tense, fragile truce with the protagonists, Queen Aphyrosia, and Chief V to solve the mystery of the Temple of Crystals and avert a global disaster.

Appearance

Dr. Thorne is described as having gaunt features and an intensely sharp gaze. He wears a coat bearing the faded insignia of the Kingston medi-laboratory and keeps his hands covered with gloves. His posture is stiff, commanding, and perpetually authoritative.

Character Arc/Goal

Thorne’s immediate goal is to prevent a planet-wide cataclysm. He discovered that the massive crystals within the temple are acting as overcharged arcane capacitors on the brink of bursting, which would destabilize the global arcane field and trigger apocalyptic storms. To stop this, he seeks to unlock the secrets of the Sent Ones, utilizing Lucas and Mina as the "keys."

Secret

Despite his villainous past, his current warning is absolutely genuine. Lucas performed a surface-level mind read on Thorne and found no deception—only complex calculations, simulations of global devastation, and a desperate, weary urgency to avert the crisis. Thorne is not lying about the impending doom.

Combat Training

Thorne is a non-combatant. He relies entirely on Kael’s lethal expertise to protect him in hostile environments, issuing swift, cold commands to his bodyguard if he feels physically or mentally threatened.

Inventory

Thorne is heavily equipped with cutting-edge magi-tech. He operates a compact, ruggedized mobile laboratory pod featuring an array of multi-layered holographic displays, a datapad, and several hover-drones capable of emitting and scanning precise frequencies of light and arcane energy. In the past, he was noted carrying small vials of shimmering liquid used to sedate/mutate his test subjects.

Lucas

His former victim. Thorne displays no remorse for torturing Lucas, viewing him merely as an essential tool to fulfill the prophecy and stop the Disciples. Despite this, the two form an incredibly effective, if hateful, problem-solving duo inside the temple.

Kael

Thorne's hired mercenary and bodyguard. Thorne trusts Kael to execute his defense perfectly, casually ordering him to prioritize his safety if Lucas attempts to telepathically compromise his mind.

Elara (Historian)

Thorne's hired academic. He relies on Elara to provide historical context to the physical anomalies he detects, demonstrating a pragmatic willingness to delegate tasks outside his scientific expertise.
