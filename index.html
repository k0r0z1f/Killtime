Welcome to Killtime

Enter a world where the boundaries of magic and technology collapse, where ancient forces collide with the rush of progress, and where the ticking clock holds more than just the passage of time.

Killtime takes you on a thrilling journey alongside Lucas, Mina, and their allies as they navigate a universe on the brink of chaos. In a world fractured by political intrigue, shifting allegiances, and dark secrets, they must uncover the truth about their powers, confront forgotten legacies, and survive a web of danger that threatens to destroy everything they’ve known.

From the bustling streets of Kingston to the icy peaks of the Dwarven Mountains, and beyond, the stakes couldn’t be higher. As time runs out, their choices will shape the fate of their world—if they can stay ahead of those who would use them as pawns in a game they don’t fully understand.

Are you ready to explore a world where every second counts?

Welcome to Killtime—the clock is ticking.

The Anomaly of Hybris

The name Hybris holds an absolute, terrifying weight in the universe. On a macro level, it is the terrestrial planet where the primary narrative is anchored—a world marked by stark landscapes, dormant magic, and oppressive structural governance by the World Police under the shadow of the Central Galactic Empire's legacy.

However, to a quantum genius like Lucas, Hybris is an "orphan word." It possesses absolutely no in-universe etymology or linguistic roots. It is an anomaly deliberately inserted by the Architect—an undeniable, mathematical proof to Lucas that his reality is a constructed narrative. A thermodynamic friction point between the creator and the created.

The Hybris Standard Calendar (HSC)

Established Year 0 HSC by the Founders' Scientific Council following the arrival of the exiled.
Current Year: 1772 HSC

Year Structure

  • The year consists of 10 Months.
  • Each Month consists of 3 Weeks.
  • Each Week consists of 10 Days.
  • Each Day consists of 24 Hours.

(Total Days per Month: 30  |  Total Days per Year: 300)

Month Names

Named after prominent scientists and researchers from the founding council.

  1. Aris
  2. Borin
  3. Cygnus
  4. Delia
  5. Elara
  6. Feron
  7. Galen
  8. Heston
  9. Ilias
  10. Jorvan

Week Structure & Day Naming

Weeks are identified by suffixes related to the phases of Hybris's most prominent moon. 10 base Day Names cycle through the weeks:

  • Solus, Duon, Trion, Quadra, Penta, Hexa, Septa, Octa, Nova, Deca

Week Suffixes (Moon Cycle Concept)

  • Week 1 Suffix: -cres (Representing the Crescent or waxing/growing phase)
  • Week 2 Suffix: -plen (Representing the Plena or full/peak phase)
  • Week 3 Suffix: -wan (Representing the Waning or decreasing phase)

Example Output

If an event occurred on the 5th day of the 2nd week of the 7th month in the year 1772, it is recorded as:

Penta-plen, Galen 1772 HSC

The Core Triad

The thermodynamic friction of Timeline-A relies entirely on the interplay of three distinct entities. The math is absolute.

Lucas
The Calculating Void
Traumatized lab rat. Quantum genius. His mind is an endless procession of geometric calibration and chilling logic. In the face of danger, he initiates a suffocating threat assessment, stripping the warmth from reality to map every fracture and feedback loop.
Mina
The Living Current
A torrent of untamed heat held in check. She moves with a feral grace, her body a coiled spring of relentless velocity. She is the vital spark, the pulse that ignites the suffocating math of the void, translating instinct into a devastating cascade.
Thomas
The Iron Foundation
The bedrock. Immovable mass. When the kinetic storm threatens to tear reality apart, he stands as the tectonic bulwark. Immune to the shifting currents of magic, his unyielding presence anchors the triad, bearing the heavy, crushing toll of survival.

*Killtime* is a passion project born from over 30 years of imaginative worldbuilding and storytelling, now brought to life with the aid of modern tools. For decades, this intricate universe—rich with layered characters, complex prophecies, and dynamic conflicts—existed solely in my mind. Today, I act as both its creator and director, using advanced AI tools like ChatGPT to refine narratives, deepen character arcs, and develop a cohesive structure.

These tools have transformed what was once an overwhelming endeavor into an achievable reality, allowing me to approach the project with precision and vision. As a one-person creative force, AI technology has enabled me to finally share this expansive world. In the future, I dream of collaborating with real artists to create renditions of pivotal scenes and character portraits, bringing *Killtime* to life visually as well as narratively.
