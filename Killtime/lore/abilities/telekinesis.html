Mapping the geometric logic and gravitational manipulation of the Telekinesis sequence.

Phase I: The Sterile Metric & The Panopticon [RC1 Data]

1761 to 1770 HSC - The Central Institute (Clinical Confinement)

During the years of isolated experimentation, Lucas-A's telekinesis was conditioned to operate strictly as a sterile metric. He views space not as an empty void, but as a rigid grid of interlocking variables. Moving an object is not a mystical exertion of willpower; it is a clinical calculation of mass, trajectory, and necessary kinetic input. He manipulates the local gravity around a target by hacking the physical laws of the immediate grid, requiring dense psychological firewalls to prevent sensory overload from the sheer volume of spatial data continuously filtering into his mind.

System Note: The foundational algorithm. His telekinesis is cold and absolute. It is less about forcefully pushing matter and more about meticulously rewriting the geometric coordinates of reality itself.
1771 HSC - The Archive Leak

When his emotional equilibrium is shattered by the sudden proximity of Mina, the clinical precision of his mind fails. A heavy oak door is violently blown off its hinges by a raw, uncalculated telekinetic pulse. The untamed heat of her presence overwhelms his concentration, bypassing the safety valves of his mind and causing an immediate, localized spatial distortion without a governing equation.

Power Progression: The first crack in the firewall. Pure emotional impulse temporarily replaces calculated logic, resulting in uncontrolled, catastrophic force that ignores his standard mathematical limits.
