Entity Resonance Log: Neural Architecture (Lucas-A)

Mapping the psychological firewalls and frequency manipulation of solitary telepathy.

Phase I: The Acoustic Void & Psychological Firewalls [RC1 Data]

1761 to 1770 HSC - The Central Institute (Clinical Confinement)

Subjected to extreme sensory saturation during his captivity, Lucas-A constructed a highly fortified neural architecture just to survive the sheer volume of unfiltered thought radiating from the laboratory. His solitary telepathy is rarely an empathetic connection; it is a rigid system of active firewalls and frequency filters. To read a mind is to execute a brute-force decryption of an external neural network. He perceives organic thoughts as chaotic algorithms, requiring cold, mathematical focus to isolate variables without suffering psychological contamination.

System Note: The primary defense mechanism. He initially leverages telepathy to construct an acoustic void, actively deleting the static of lesser minds from his perception to preserve the absolute integrity of his own geometric calculus.

Phase II: Invasive Decryption & Tactical Hacking [RC1 Data]

1772 HSC - Tactical Application

Outside the controlled environment of the laboratory, his telepathic application becomes a tool for immediate data extraction. When interrogating a target, he does not gently coax information; he systematically dismantles their psychological defenses. By identifying the exact frequency of their fear or trauma, he injects a dissonant psychic signal, forcing their neural architecture to collapse and yield the required data without physical contact.

System Note: The sterile violation. The process is devoid of empathy or emotional resonance. He views the host mind purely as a compromised database to be mined and subsequently discarded.

Phase III: The Vulnerability & The Phantom Echoes [Draft Data]

1772 HSC - The Subterranean Crucible

During the confrontation with higher-dimensional anomalies, the limitations of his rigid firewalls are brutally exposed. Against entities like the Disciples or the Minulican, his attempts to decrypt their neural frequencies result in catastrophic feedback. Their minds are built on cosmic mathematics that far exceed his local terrestrial algorithms. Attempting to force a connection without Mina's stabilizing heat results in severe neural hemorrhaging, as his brain attempts to process an infinite recursion of variables.

Power Progression: The hardware limit is reached. His cold calculus is unparalleled against mortal minds, but attempting to hack the River of Time's agents causes his own geometric grids to splinter and burn.
