Background Summary (Pre-Institute)

A high school football player and fiercely loyal friend. Thomas is entirely unaware of the arcane reality hidden beneath the world. He simply goes to class, plays sports, and tries to keep his isolated friend Lucas grounded.

He has zero active magical capacity. However, his biology houses a singular, latent arcane ability that he is entirely unaware of: an "Acoustic Void" synced specifically to Lucas and Mina's arcane signatures. To them, his presence actively nullifies their magical radiation, acting as a massive, immovable dampener that allows Lucas's overloaded mind to rest.
