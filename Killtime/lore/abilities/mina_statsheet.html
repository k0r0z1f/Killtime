Background Summary (Pre-Institute)

Raised in a gilded cage orchestrated by her Uncle, Mina has spent her life running—literally. Anchored heavily in the absolute present, her latent kinetic magic bleeds unconsciously into her devastating athletic performance. She is unaware of her true arcane potential, viewing her untamed heat merely as adrenaline.

She serves as the emotional thermostat for Lucas. Where his world is a freezing, geometric void, hers is a chaotic, breathing torrent of vitality. At this stage, her magic is dormant, constrained by the societal pressure of her elite upbringing.
