Birth Year

Kael's exact birth year is unknown. Given his lean muscle, combat readiness, and status as an elite mercenary, he is likely a hardened adult in his physical prime.

Role/Background

Kael is a private mercenary and highly disciplined bodyguard hired by Dr. Thorne (the rogue researcher from the Kingston medi-laboratory). He accompanied Thorne and the historian Elara on their illicit, forced excavation into the sealed Temple of Crystals within the Watcher mountain. His sole purpose is to act as the physical muscle for Thorne's academic expedition, ensuring that the "conversation remains civil" when confronted by the heavily armed protagonist party.

Personality Traits

Kael is profoundly silent, displaying absolute professional discipline. He does not speak, complain, or react emotionally to the majestic environment of the Temple of Crystals. He is completely detached from the academic and prophetic implications of the ruins, focusing entirely on threat assessment. His posture is relaxed but constantly alert, possessing the cold pragmatism of someone thoroughly accustomed to conflict.

Abilities/Magic

There is no evidence that Kael uses magic. Instead, he represents a modern, physical threat. He relies on advanced modern weaponry (a sidearm/firearm) in a world where many still fight with swords and elemental magic. His primary ability is his hyper-vigilance and lightning-fast reaction time to shifts in the room's tension.

Alliances/Clans/Factions

Kael's loyalty is strictly transactional; he is contracted to Dr. Thorne and the remnants of the Kingston regime. As such, he stands in direct opposition to the Children of Hybris, the Nythari Tribe, and the Elven Envoy, though he only initiates hostility if his employer is threatened.

Appearance

Kael has a leanly muscled build that speaks of a life of constant physical conditioning and combat. He wears practical, dark armor that is a unique blend of modern and archaic design, allowing for mobility while offering heavy protection. His face is entirely obscured by a tactical helmet, turning him into a faceless, intimidating enforcer.

Character Arc/Goal

Kael does not harbor grand prophetic goals. His immediate, uncompromising objective is to keep Dr. Thorne alive at all costs. During the tense standoff in the Temple of Crystals, his goal is to establish a perimeter of deterrence, silently warning Thomas, Altheris, and Lucas that any sudden moves will be met with lethal force.

Secret

Kael's identity is entirely hidden beneath his helmet. Notably, he shares a name with a Resistance scout from the Atraxia sewers (Chapter 30). Whether this is a coincidence, an alias, or a sign of a dual identity/betrayal remains an unspoken mystery surrounding the mercenary.

Combat Training

He is an expert in VIP protection and modern combat. His relaxed but ready stance indicates he is a veteran of high-stress scenarios. When Thorne issues a subtle command regarding Lucas, Kael’s posture shifts seamlessly into a targeted alert phase, keeping his hand perfectly hovered over his sidearm to guarantee the fastest possible draw speed.

Inventory

Kael is equipped with custom dark hybrid-armor, a face-concealing combat helmet, and a holstered modern sidearm (likely a firearm/pistol) resting on his hip, distinguishing him from the blade-wielding vanguards of the main party.

Dr. Thorne

His current employer. Kael follows Thorne’s verbal instructions instantly. When Thorne warns Lucas to stop probing his mind, he instructs Kael to prioritize his well-being if he starts acting unusually. Kael acknowledges this with a silent dip of his helmet.

Lucas

Kael recognizes Lucas as the primary, unpredictable threat in the room due to his telepathy and magic. At Thorne's command, Kael locks his focused, unyielding alertness squarely onto Lucas.

Thomas & Altheris

Kael serves as a mutual deterrent against the party’s physical protectors. Thomas grips his sword while staring at Kael, recognizing the mercenary as the main obstacle preventing the party from simply overpowering the arrogant Dr. Thorne.
