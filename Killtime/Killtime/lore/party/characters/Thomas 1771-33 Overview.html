Birth Year

Thomas was born in 1753, making him 17 years old at the time of the story. Despite his youth, his massive stature and heavily grounded maturity make him appear much older to those who meet him.

Role/Background

Thomas is the physical anchor and primary vanguard of the group. Known briefly as the Hero of Brum'korath, he is a former student turned fugitive after the Kingston incident. He is marked as an enemy of the state by the World Police. During the treacherous ascent of the Watcher, Thomas protected the party from the Beast and the Minulican. Most recently, deep within the "Tombs of the First" in the Temple of Crystals, Thomas faced a staggering revelation: he found an ancient obsidian sarcophagus bearing his own name, Thomar.

Personality Traits

Thomas is heavily pragmatic, stoic, and fiercely protective. He operates as the group's grounded center of gravity, highly skeptical of cryptic magic, riddles, and prophecies (often rolling his eyes at the Elven Queen and Ceylan). He reacts to danger with immediate, decisive force. While he masks his anxiety with sarcasm and a tough exterior, he is deeply burdened by the escalating danger his friends are in. He demonstrated his hyper-protective instincts when he furiously intercepted a boulder meant for Candice after Lucas and Mina's magic misfired.

Abilities/Magic

  • Relic Mastery (Piston-Gauntlet): Unlike Lucas and Mina's dormant relics, Thomas's relic is fully "awake" and "complete." It fluidly reshapes itself over his arm into a bulky, piston-like gauntlet that absorbs massive impacts (like shattering a flying boulder) and delivers devastating kinetic shockwaves.
  • Relic Sentience: Thomas has discovered that his relic is a highly intelligent, ancient entity. It can communicate with him, though it refuses to initiate conversation. The relic is manipulative, feeding him riddles (e.g., "One truth, one lie") and faking system malfunctions to avoid answering direct questions about its origins.
  • Superhuman Endurance & Strength: Capable of withstanding blows from massive creatures like the Guardian Beast and hurling enemies across rooms.

Alliances/Clans/Factions

Thomas is unwaveringly loyal to Lucas and Mina. He holds a cautious, purely pragmatic alliance with Queen Aph and the forces of Haliriel, relying on them for survival but trusting none of their politics. He is a direct enemy of the World Police and the Empire.

Appearance

Standing at an imposing 6'5", Thomas has a broad, athletic frame built for taking and giving heavy damage. He dresses practically for travel and combat. His most notable feature is the shifting metallic relic fused to his arm, which visibly reacts and reshapes itself based on his combat needs and emotional state.

Character Arc/Goal

Thomas’s arc revolves around his transition from a standard protector into a warrior wielding a god-like, manipulative weapon. His goal is to safeguard his friends as their own powers become more dangerous and volatile. Moving forward, he must navigate a battle of wills against his sentient relic while coming to terms with his apparent reincarnation or lineage tied to the ancient Sent One, "Thomar."

Secret

He harbors complex inner conflicts about the morality of their war and his growing isolation as Lucas and Mina's bond deepens. More recently, his biggest secret involves his relic: he knows it is a fully conscious, manipulative entity claiming to be "the last creation of the Gods." It fed Lucas a terrifying riddle—that the Sent Ones were never meant to return—casting heavy doubt on their entire destiny.

Combat Training

Thomas relies on heavy vanguard tactics, acting as a physical shield for his squishier allies. He seamlessly blends sword and shield mastery with brutal, relic-enhanced brawling. His reaction time is exceptional, allowing him to instinctively place himself in the path of lethal attacks meant for others.

Inventory

Thomas carries 86 doumis, a heavy travel backpack, standard survival gear, his trusted sword and shield, and the highly advanced, sentient piston-gauntlet relic fused to his arm.

Lucas

His best friend. Thomas acts as his physical anchor and protector. While he trusts Lucas implicitly, he is growing increasingly wary of how volatile and destructive Lucas's magic is becoming.

Mina

A trusted ally. Thomas relies on Mina's speed to flank enemies while he draws their fire. He was furious at her when a combined magical stunt with Lucas went wrong, showing he holds her to a high standard of control.

Candice

A relatively new acquaintance for whom Thomas has developed a subtle, unconscious crush. His immediate, reflexive dive to block a shattered boulder from hitting her highlights his protective instinct and growing affection for her.

Dira

A newer friend in the group. Thomas views her as a non-combatant who requires his protection, often keeping her behind his shield line during hostile encounters.
