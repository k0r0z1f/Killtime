Birth Year

Candice was born in the year 1721. At 49 years old, her age reflects the era she comes from and the extensive experience she brings as a seasoned warrior, navigator, and tribal guide.

Role/Background

Candice is a dedicated and highly respected member of the Nythari tribe. As a warrior, messenger, and seeker, she has been entrusted with crucial missions, chief among them guiding the prophesied Children of Hybris to the Watcher. After traversing treacherous mountain paths and surviving the Guardian Beast's lair, she reunited with her tribe under Chief V. She actively led the physical and tactical efforts to excavate the collapsed Temple of Crystals following the rogue Researcher's intrusion. Recently, her most devastating trial was finding her lifelong mentor, Ceylan, reduced to a rambling, incoherent madman.

Personality Traits

Candice is agile, confident, pragmatic, and highly determined. Her personality is marked by a fierce loyalty to her Nythari heritage and a deep reverence for the sacred history of her people. While generally calm and commanding in wilderness survival, recent events have shown her vulnerability: she is profoundly shaken and devastated by Ceylan's mental decline. She remains deeply resourceful and observant, prioritizing the mission and the safety of her companions over her own emotional turmoil.

Abilities/Magic

Skilled in survival techniques, Candice excels at vertical climbing, stealth, and archery (having used her last arrow in the fight against the Beast). She possesses an uncanny environmental intuition, easily detecting unnatural shifts in the mountain's terrain and recognizing ancient Nythari script hidden in the ruins. An enigmatic mental presence—an authoritative, chaotic parasite placed by her mentor Ceylan—subtly accompanies her thoughts, though its full purpose remains a mystery.

Alliances/Clans/Factions

Candice is deeply allied with her homeland, the Nythari Tribe, and respects the authority of Chief V. She aligns with the Elven Queen Aphyrosia, trusting her ancient wisdom. In her immediate group, she serves as the crucial navigator for Lucas, Mina, Thomas, Dira, and Alt, ensuring the expedition survives the deadly environment of the Watcher.

Appearance

Candice has an athletic build with striking, bright amber eyes and long brown hair woven into a practical braid. Dressed in durable, earthy Nythari warrior attire and a thick cloak suited for the biting cold of the mountains, she carries an aura of cultural pride. She currently bears minor injuries, including a blood-stained gash on her side from a near-miss encounter with the Guardian Beast.

Character Arc/Goal

Candice’s journey centers on uncovering the truth behind the prophecy and protecting her tribe's sacred lands. Her goal shifted from simply guiding Lucas and Mina to the Watcher, to actively reclaiming the desecrated Temple of Crystals. Moving forward, she must reconcile the traumatic loss of her mentor’s sanity and navigate the dangerously escalating arcane powers of her companions.

Secret

Candice harbors a chaotic, parasitic mental presence linked to Ceylan—a secret Lucas uncovered through telepathy. Recently, she narrowly survived a fatal accident when a highly volatile, synchronized telekinetic blast from Lucas and Mina sent a massive boulder hurtling at her head. She hides her lingering anxiety about how dangerous her prophesied companions are becoming.

Combat Training

Trained extensively in survival, climbing, stealth, and archery. She is adept in fast-paced combat scenarios against primal creatures, utilizing hit-and-run arrow tactics. Her proficiency extends to the use of blow darts and short blades, making her an expert in both offense and reconnaissance during dangerous expeditions.

Inventory

Candice carries an empty bow, a dagger in a holster, a pouch of iridescent stones (currency), a small stone fragment from Nythari ruins, and essential climbing/survival gear. She recently acquired two highly significant items from the Beast's lair: a darkened metal plate etched with faded ancient Nythari script, and a jagged crystal shard containing a hostile, hateful soul (wrapped tightly in leather).

Lucas

Recognizes him as a Child of Hybris. While she respects him and guided him to the Watcher, she is currently wary after his and Mina's poorly controlled telekinetic blast nearly killed her.

Mina

Recognizes her as a Child of Hybris. Candice maintains a strong mutual respect for Mina's agility and courage in battle, though they share the awkward fallout of the recent reckless excavation blast.

Thomas

Candice owes her life to Thomas. During the chaotic breach of the Temple of Crystals, Thomas used his relic-bound arm to shatter a massive rock flying directly at Candice's head. She views him as an indispensable and deeply protective shield for the group.

Ceylan

Her mentor and the former guardian of the Watcher. Discovering that Ceylan’s mind has completely unraveled into madness and riddles has left Candice heartbroken, fundamentally altering her understanding of the prophecy.
