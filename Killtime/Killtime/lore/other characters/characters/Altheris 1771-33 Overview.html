Birth Year

No birth year details are provided for Altheris, but as a high-ranking Elven commander with deep battle experience, he likely possesses the long lifespan characteristic of his people.

Role/Background

Altheris (often called "Alt") serves as the Commandant of Haliriel's Defense. He acted as the official Elven Envoy to the Dwarven Council in Brum'korath, successfully negotiating diplomatic asylum and extraction for Lucas, Mina, and Thomas. He is the designated commander for the upcoming Eastern Continent expedition via the Windfighter. During the treacherous ascent of the Watcher and the descent into the Temple of Crystals, he acts as the expedition's steadfast rear guard, ensuring no threats approach them from behind.

Personality Traits

Altheris is defined by his unwavering composure, pragmatic stoicism, and vigilant nature. As a diplomat, he displays calm restraint and unyielding confidence, gracefully navigating the heavy resentment and racism of the Dwarven Council. As a soldier, he is highly observant, keeping his emotions unreadable but maintaining a constant readiness to draw his blade to protect his Queen and his charges.

Abilities/Magic

He is highly proficient in swordsmanship and military tactics. While he has not displayed innate magical spellcasting, he possesses an expert understanding of advanced Elven hybrid-technology, seamlessly blending arcane engineering with practical field operations (such as operating the Windfighter and magi-tech recording devices).

Alliances/Clans/Factions

He is fundamentally loyal to Haliriel and Queen Aphyrosia, prioritizing her safety above all else. He has formed a functioning, protective alliance with the Children of Hybris (Lucas and Mina) and their companions. He actively distrusts Thorne and the mercenary Kael, viewing them as unpredictable threats to the mission.

Appearance

Altheris presents an imposing yet graceful elven figure. He has silver hair, sharp pale green eyes, and a muscular, battle-hardened build adorned with fresh scars from recent conflicts. During diplomatic and official missions, he wears an elegant tunic heavily embroidered with intricate patterns of vines and stars, blending military functionality with royal Elven aesthetics.

Character Arc/Goal

His primary goal is to execute the will of Haliriel: securing the Children of Hybris, extracting answers regarding the Disciple of Air, and commanding the upcoming Eastern Continent expedition. In the immediate present, his arc revolves around protecting his Queen and the party as they uncover the ancient, world-altering secrets buried in the Temple of Crystals and the Tombs of the First.

Secret

Altheris spotted the massive, hairy Guardian Beast stalking the group's camp long before it attacked. Following Queen Aphyrosia's direct orders, he kept this sighting a secret from the rest of the party to prevent panic, bearing the burden of vigilance alone.

Combat Training

He is an elite, military-trained Elven commander. In battle, he acts as a disciplined interceptor and rear guard, stepping directly into the path of massive beasts to shield more vulnerable party members (such as Candice and Dira). Even when grievously injured, his combat endurance allows him to maintain his defensive line.

Inventory

He carries a primary fencing sword and a long blade strapped to his back for heavy combat. Additionally, he utilizes a small, highly advanced hybrid-tech camera device from the Elven realms, which he used to document the impossible, starless galaxy they witnessed in the vanished mansion.

Queen Aphyrosia

His monarch and liege. Altheris is fiercely loyal to her, acting as her personal shield. He will instinctively throw himself in front of lethal magic or physical blows to ensure her survival.

The Party (Lucas, Mina, Thomas)

He maintains a professional, protective stance toward the trio. While he respects Thomas's combat pragmatism and Lucas's power, he views them primarily as vital assets to Haliriel's mission that must be guarded at all costs.

Dira

A quiet, mutual respect exists between Altheris and Dira. After he sustained a severe blow to the chest from the Guardian Beast, Dira scolded him for hiding the bleeding and skillfully stitched his wounds, an act he accepted with stoic approval.

Thorne & Kael

Altheris views the rogue researcher and his mercenary bodyguard with extreme suspicion. Upon discovering them deep within the Temple of Crystals, Altheris immediately rested his hand on his sword hilt, preparing to execute them if they made a hostile move toward his Queen or the party.
